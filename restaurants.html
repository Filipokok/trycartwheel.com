FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Hybrid Delivery?

Hybrid Delivery means running your own drivers and third-party providers at the same time, with rules deciding which orders go where. Your drivers take the orders where control and margin matter. Third-party providers absorb the rest.

Is Cartwheel a delivery service like DoorDash?

No. Cartwheel does not employ drivers or deliver orders. It is the software that runs your delivery operation, including your own drivers and third-party providers such as Uber Direct, DoorDash Drive, and Grubhub.

Which rules can I set?

Time of day, order size, driver availability, order throttling, delivery radius and zones, and pickup ETA comparison. Rules work together, so one policy covers every shift and location.

What does Cartwheel integrate with?

POS and ordering systems including Toast, PAR, Olo, Qu, and ChowNow; catering platforms including ezCater; and delivery providers including Uber Direct, DoorDash Drive, Grubhub, Skipcart, and 50+ others.

How much does Cartwheel cost?

Pricing starts at $65 per location per month in the US, with volume-based and enterprise pricing available.
