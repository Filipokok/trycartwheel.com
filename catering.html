FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Hybrid Delivery for catering?

Your own drivers take the catering orders where service and tips matter most, and vetted catering fleets cover the rest. Rules decide which orders go where, so no one sorts them by hand.

Does Cartwheel meet ezCater's tracking requirements?

Yes. Self-delivered ezCater orders tracked through Cartwheel's ezCater Delivery API integration meet ezCater's tracking standard, which applies to every ezCater order from March 31, 2026. For third-party coverage, request an ezDispatch driver from the Cartwheel dashboard.

Which catering platforms does Cartwheel integrate with?

ezCater, Olo, and Toast Catering, plus your own website, app, POS, and phone orders. Every catering order lands on one board.

Can Cartwheel handle scheduled catering orders?

Yes. Scheduled orders are dispatched at the right time automatically, with order checklists, photo and signature proof of delivery, and live tracking for the customer.

Which third-party fleets deliver catering?

DoorDash through its Large Order Program, Uber Direct, and 50+ catering-trained local fleets.

How much does Cartwheel cost for catering delivery?

Pricing for catering starts at $50 per location per month in the US, with volume-based and enterprise pricing available.
